Research Domain & Methodology

Exploring the scientific domain, research gaps, problem statement, core objectives, machine learning methodology, and underlying technology stack for localized climate decision support in Colombo District.

01. DOMAIN BACKGROUND

Literature Survey

Climate change in Sri Lanka has intensified extreme weather events, leading to severe urban flooding, fluctuating rainfall patterns affecting agricultural yield, rapid spread of vector-borne diseases such as Dengue, and disruption of transportation routes during emergency weather events.

Existing disaster response mechanisms primarily rely on macro-level national forecasts or manual reporting. Literature shows that high-density urban zones such as Colombo District require localized micro-level predictions combining multiple environmental parameters (rainfall, river gauge levels, mosquito breeding timelines, traffic density, and soil conditions) to empower authorities and citizens with actionable real-time risk intelligence.

02. IDENTIFIED LIMITATIONS

Research Gap

Lack of Localized Granularity

Current climate warning systems provide generalized district or provincial warnings, lacking specific division-wise precision necessary for targeted early response in Colombo District.

Isolated Single-Domain Systems

Most solutions deal with weather, river level, and travel information separately, without one integrated AI decision support framework that turns this data into clear, localized advice.

03. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Research Problem

"Communities, authorities, and farmers in Sri Lanka face severe climate-related risks due to the absence of a localized, unified AI-driven decision support system that converts live environmental data into early warnings and actionable advice."

Project Problem & Expected Impact

Addressing critical gaps in current climate warning mechanisms across Sri Lanka.

Identified Problems

Key vulnerabilities in existing warning systems

  • People do not receive early flood warnings in time to protect life and property.
  • People are not aware of the climate risk levels in their immediate surrounding areas.
  • Farmers do not receive specific advice related to weather and flood conditions.
  • People find it difficult to identify safe travel routes during sudden urban floods.

Expected Impacts

Positive outcomes delivered by our AI solution

  • Improves public safety through timely disaster notifications.
  • Increases public awareness of high-risk divisions in their area.
  • Supports local communities and farmers with location-based AI advice.
  • Helps citizens identify safer travel routes during severe weather events.
04. PROJECT GOALS

Research Objectives

Main Objective: Develop a localized, unified AI-driven climate-aware decision support and risk forecasting system for Colombo District, Sri Lanka, that turns live environmental data into early warnings and actionable advice for the public, authorities, and farmers.

  • Integrate live weather, river level, and map data into a single localized data pipeline for Colombo District.
  • Train and compare machine learning models (XGBoost and Random Forest) to identify the most accurate model for climate risk prediction.
  • Deliver division-wise risk forecasts and early warning alerts through a mobile application.
  • Provide AI-generated, easy-to-understand advice and safe travel guidance during adverse weather events.
05. SYSTEM PIPELINE

Methodology

The system uses a modular machine learning workflow where each of the four components captures its own data, transforms it into meaningful indicators, and sends those outputs into one final integrated decision support layer.

01

Flood Prediction

The flood module gathers live rainfall and river water level data. These inputs go through preprocessing and feature engineering, then an XGBoost classification process. The system classifies the flood risk level for each Colombo District division.

RainfallRiver levelsFlood risk level
02

Dengue Early Warning

The dengue module combines climate data with historical dengue case records. After preprocessing and feature engineering, machine learning models learn the relationship between weather patterns and outbreaks, and forecast division-wise dengue risk for the coming weeks.

Climate dataCase recordsRisk forecast
03

Paddy Advisory

The paddy module collects weather and field condition data, predicts yield and flood or drought risk, and then sends the results to the Gemini AI API, which writes simple, location-based farming advice for farmers.

WeatherYield & riskAI advice
04

Safe Routing

The routing module uses the Google Maps API for road networks and combines them with flood risk outputs and weather conditions. Affected or blocked roads are avoided, and the system recommends safer alternative routes during adverse weather.

INTEGRATED DECISION SUPPORT LAYER

Risk Forecasting & Advisory Delivery

Finally, the outputs from all four components are combined and processed by a FastAPI backend hosted on Railway, then delivered through the React Native Expo mobile app as division-wise risk maps, early warning alerts, AI-generated advice, and safe route guidance.
